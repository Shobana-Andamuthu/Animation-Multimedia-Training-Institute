Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about enrollment, lab hardware, and installment fee payments.

No prior drawing or 3D software background is required for our foundational diploma programs. Our curriculum begins with classical pencil sketching, human anatomy, and basic computer graphics theory before advancing into Maya, Houdini, and Unreal Engine.
AniLearn provides fully equipped studio workstations with RTX-accelerated GPUs, Wacom Cintiq 4K displays, and official studio software licenses. Students also receive educational cloud access licenses for remote practice on their personal laptops.
We offer regular weekday morning and afternoon tracks, as well as dedicated weekend batches for working professionals. Course fees can be paid in zero-interest monthly installments across the duration of the program.
Upon finishing your capstone project, our placement team assists you with demo reel optimization, resume curation, and mock technical tests. We then schedule direct recruitment interviews with delegates from our network of 200+ partner studios.
